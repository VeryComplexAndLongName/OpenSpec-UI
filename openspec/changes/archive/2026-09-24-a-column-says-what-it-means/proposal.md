## Why

Reported by the owner on 2026-09-23: in the Pipeline, a change with 13 of
13 tasks done sits under the heading "Step 1 - can start now" (drawn with a middle dot then). What is
there to start?

Nothing, and the heading never meant there was. The first column holds
the changes that nothing active blocks - its place in the declared order.
"Can start now" names an invitation instead of a place, and is true only
of a change nobody has started. For one that is running, done, or failed,
it reads as advice to start it again.

## What Changes

- The first column is headed "Step 1 - waits for nothing", the others
  "Step N - after step N-1": the separator becomes a plain hyphen, as
  every word this product writes is plain ASCII. Both words say where a change stands in the
  order, which is true of any change in the column whatever it is doing.
- "Waits for" is the legend's own word: a line between two cards means
  the second waits for the first.

## Explicitly out of scope

- **The arrangement by stage.** The board heads its columns with the
  stages' own words; nothing there invites a start.
- **`openspec-ui-cli ready`.** It answers what can start now, and that is
  what it lists: changes nothing blocks and nobody has finished.
