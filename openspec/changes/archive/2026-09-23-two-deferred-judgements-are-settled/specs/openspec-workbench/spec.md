## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A settled judgement says what settled it

A deferred judgement that is ticked in the workspace's list SHALL say, on
its own line, what settled it: what was seen or done, and when or where.
A tick alone SHALL NOT stand: a judgement is about a shipped thing, and a
month later the line is all there is of it.

#### Scenario: A judgement the product answered

- **WHEN** a judgement asking whether the sweep rebases a behind branch
  is ticked
- **THEN** its line names what the sweep did, on what, and when it was
  seen
