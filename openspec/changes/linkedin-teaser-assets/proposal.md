# LinkedIn Teaser Assets

## Why

The teaser describes user-visible behavior from the shared-core delivery model
in ADR 0001, but its image placeholders currently have no project-owned visual
assets. Adding product-shaped visuals makes the write-up publishable without
inventing screenshots that claim to show a state the UI does not expose.

## Capabilities

### New

- Four standalone SVG visuals for the teaser's permission, Human-Only Inbox,
  human observation, and mechanical-check scenes.

### Modified

- None.

## Scope

The assets are editorial graphics only. They do not change runtime behavior,
package contracts, or the standalone and extension delivery targets.
