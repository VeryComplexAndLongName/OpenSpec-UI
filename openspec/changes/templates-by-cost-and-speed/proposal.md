# Name the configurations by what a person is choosing between

## Why

The three named configurations are called Careful, Overnight and
Thrifty. That names them by how closely a person watches — which is one
consequence of the choice, not the choice itself.

Someone picking a configuration is deciding between spending less,
finishing sooner, and something in between. The figures that answer that
are already in each template, in a `basis` sentence below the fold. The
titles do not carry them, so the axis a person actually reasons on is the
one the list does not show.

Reported by the owner, who asked for exactly this axis and found the
shipped names on a different one. The record shows the axis was never
argued: `settings-templates`'s design records two rejected alternatives
and neither is this, so it was chosen rather than decided.

## Capabilities

### Modified

- The named configurations are titled by cost and time, and each states
  its ceilings where they are read rather than in prose below.

## Out of scope

Changing what any configuration does. The three sets of ceilings were
measured against this repository's own audit log, and the measurement is
unchanged — this renames the axis they are presented on and puts the
figures where the choice is made.

Adding a fourth. Three named intents make the trade visible; a fourth
makes it a list to study.
