## Post

A checkout feature spans the web app and the API. The requirements live in a third repository. Which local checkout should you open to review the postal-code contract?

**Show OpenSpec Locations** in Workbench brings your registered OpenSpec Stores and Worksets into the workspace view. Open a store to browse the specs in that checkout. Open a workset to see the folders you normally use together.

The example starts with a planning repository and two code repositories. It shows where to find the spec before reviewing a change in either app.

Full write-up: https://openspec-ui.dev/articles/where-the-shared-spec-lives/
