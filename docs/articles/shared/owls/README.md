# Owls for article covers

Six poses of the OpenSpec Workbench owl, one for each of the next six articles, so that
a series of covers is recognisably one series. They are the round mark, cropped from a
1254 by 1254 sheet of six illustrations supplied by the owner.

Each file is a 414 by 414 PNG with transparent corners, about 250 KB. Show one no larger
than 414 pixels: there is no more detail in it than that.

| File | The pose | Suggested for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `owl-1.png` | Three-quarter view, close | a spare, or a short post |
| `owl-2.png` | Seated on a branch under a moon, facing the reader | supervision and stopping a run |
| `owl-3.png` | Thoughtful, a wing raised to the beak | where a person decides, and what the tool does not know |
| `owl-4.png` | Circuit lines around it | architecture: the shared core and two ways to ship it |
| `owl-5.png` | Eyes closed, on a book | specifications, and what outlives a change |
| `owl-6.png` | In motion, lines behind it | releases and what is new |

The suggestions are only that: a cover is designed for its article's own title, and the
owl on it is one of these six, beside the text, as on the covers of the first two articles.

The scenes in the original sheet (the moon, the shelves, the books) are mostly cropped
away by the circle, on purpose: the round mark is what the covers, avatars and previews
share. A rectangular crop of each pose is kept by the owner outside this repository.
