The chain's spending ceiling carries its unit. `budget.maxCost` takes a
ceiling per unit of account - `{ "credits": 500 }` - checked against what
was reported in that same unit, with nothing converted and no total mixing
units. A ceiling in a unit no stage's agent is billed in is reported before
the run, beside the other ceilings that cannot act. The run budget field in
the settings says it is in dollars, and says where a ceiling in another
unit is set.
